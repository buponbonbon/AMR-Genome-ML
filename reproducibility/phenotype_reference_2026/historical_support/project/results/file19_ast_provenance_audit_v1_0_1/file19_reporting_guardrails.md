# FILE19 — AST provenance guardrails

- E2 full-cohort (525 genomes), frozen labels and scores are unchanged.
- NCBI BioSample antibiogram can be the source of the original deposited AST, NOT independent confirmation.
- Low censored MIC labeled resistant or high censored MIC labeled susceptible is a **review flag**, NOT a relabel.
- No numeric breakpoint is assumed without species, test year, standard, dose and laboratory method.
- Paper supplementary VITEK 2 / BMD, if available, must be accession matched and its measurement provenance established.
- Missing AST cannot be reported as verified phenotype. No conclusions about AST mislabelling are supported yet.
- Post-hoc source selection cannot redefine primary E2 results or prove cause of external AUROC decline.
